About me

I'm an Information Technology graduate from Al-Quds University, based in Ramallah. My work sits at three points of the same product lifecycle: building the front end, testing it, and supporting the people who use it.

On the development side I work with React, React Native and Tailwind CSS, connecting interfaces to REST APIs.
